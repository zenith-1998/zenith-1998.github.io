ACADEMIC PROFILE

Research

My research focuses on mathematical analysis and modelling of nonlinear systems, with particular emphasis on environmental and climate-related dynamics.

Nonlinear Ordinary Differential Equations

My research focuses on nonlinear differential equations, dynamical systems, and mathematical modelling, with particular interest in their applications to environmental and physical systems. My work involves studying qualitative behaviour, stability, bifurcations, and optimal control of mathematical models, including models related to CO2 emissions, forest dynamics, reforestation, and climate-related processes. I am also interested in delay differential equations, parameter estimation using real data, and sensitivity analysis of parameters influencing the dynamics of model systems.

Mathematical Modelling

Mathematical modelling involves developing and analysing mathematical frameworks to represent real-world phenomena. My work focuses on formulating nonlinear models that describe real-life phenomena and studying their behaviour to gain insights into complex environmental and physical systems.

Dynamical Systems

My research in dynamical systems focuses on understanding the qualitative behaviour of mathematical models over time, with particular emphasis on equilibrium points, stability, bifurcation, and long-term dynamics.

CO2 Emission Modelling

My research on CO2 emission modelling focuses on developing mathematical models to study the dynamics of carbon dioxide emissions and their interaction with environmental factors such as temperature, forest biomass, reforestation, and public awareness. These models are used to investigate possible strategies for reducing emissions and improving environmental sustainability.
