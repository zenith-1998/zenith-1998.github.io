TEACHING PORTFOLIO

Teaching

My teaching experience includes mathematical analysis, measure theory, calculus, algebra, linear algebra, LaTeX, Python programming, business mathematics, and business statistics. I aim to develop conceptual understanding, mathematical rigor, computational skills, and effective problem-solving abilities among students.

Teaching Approach

My teaching approach focuses on building a strong conceptual foundation before moving toward rigorous mathematical arguments, computational techniques, and practical applications. I encourage students to understand the underlying ideas behind concepts rather than relying solely on formulas and procedures. My objective is to develop analytical thinking, mathematical reasoning, computational proficiency, and effective problem-solving skills.
